How It Works

Step-by-step workflow of the digital FIFO queue

1

Student Entry

Student submits name, roll number, and selects meal type.

2

Token Allocation

System generates a unique token (e.g., M101) and enqueues the request.

3

Wait Calculation

Wait time is computed based on position and average service time (3 mins).

4

FIFO Service

First student in queue gets served first. Positions update automatically.

  • Reduces Physical Crowding: Eliminates physical queues in mess areas.
